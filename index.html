THE FEED

Data sources & methodology

1,221 records — 325 private-sector investments, 896 public-sector awards — as of the 2026-09-17 export.

Status of this export

Private-investment extraction is complete for this pilot phase. Public-investment extraction is not yet complete: federal grants (430 records) are done; the enacted state capital budget (Senate Bill 283, ~305 of an estimated 600+ line items) and federal contracts (~135 of 483 candidates at the $100M+ floor) were paused mid-extraction. Every record currently published, complete or not, meets the full sourcing/verification bar — nothing partial or unverified was included.

Federal government

SEC EDGAR (sec.gov) — full-text search across 8-K, 10-K, 10-Q, S-1, and DEF 14A filings for company disclosures of Maryland facility investments, expansions, and acquisitions.
USASpending.gov — the federal government's official public spending database: grants, cooperative agreements, and contracts awarded to Maryland-based recipients or performed in Maryland.

Maryland state government

Maryland General Assembly — Chapter 5, 2026 Laws of Maryland (Senate Bill 283, "Maryland Consolidated Capital Bond Loan of 2026"), the enacted state capital budget.
Maryland Department of Commerce (business.maryland.gov) — state economic development announcements.
Office of the Governor of Maryland (governor.maryland.gov) — gubernatorial announcements of major investments and state programs.
Good Jobs First — Subsidy Tracker (goodjobsfirst.org) — Maryland state economic-development subsidy awards, cross-referenced to the private capital investment they supported.

Maryland county & local government

Baltimore Metropolitan Council — regional building permit dataset (BPDS_2025) covering Annapolis, Anne Arundel County, Baltimore City, Baltimore County, Carroll County, Harford County, Howard County, and Queen Anne's County.
Montgomery County — commercial building permits (open data).
Prince George's County — residential, commercial, and site/road permits (open data).
Garrett County Government · Salisbury-Wicomico Economic Development (SWED)

Company sources

Direct company press releases and investor announcements, including AstraZeneca, Northrop Grumman, Mountaire Farms, Ports America, Rohde & Schwarz, Pandora Group, Logistics Property Co., Cenergy Holdings, Harbor Point/Beatty Development Group, Four Seasons, and MacroGenics, among others.

News & trade press

General/investigative: The Baltimore Banner, Baltimore Sun, Washington Post, Maryland Matters.
Trade press: BioBuzz, BioSpace, Construction Dive, Data Center Dynamics, Business Facilities Magazine, REBusinessOnline, WBOC, WMAR2 News, Fox Baltimore, Eye On Annapolis, MoCoShow, Nottingham MD, The BayNet.

Methodology notes

Every record cites at least one of the sources above (verified — no record is published without a real, checkable citation). Dollar amounts carry a confidence rating (high/medium/low) reflecting how directly the source discloses the figure. Private-sector and public-sector records are tracked separately, since they represent different decisions — but the same underlying project can appear in both, cross-linked when a public subsidy or grant helped fund a specific private investment already in the dataset. Geographic precision varies by source: every record has at least a county-level coordinate (a real Census Bureau county centroid, used as a fallback), and a subset additionally have a precise street-address-level geocode.

Data: live export from a Claude Code-run collection pipeline against the sourcing methodology and confidence rubric documented in this project's CLAUDE.md and Sourcing Playbook, exported 2026-09-15. Published without human review per an explicit pilot-phase exception. Not a complete inventory of Maryland private investment. Currently Maryland-only; "All US" will expand as additional states are added.
